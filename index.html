Privacy Policy

Wiz Auto Tap by Wiz Labs · Effective 25 November 2026

Summary

Wiz Auto Tap collects no personal data, has no accounts, and has no internet access. Nothing you do in the app is sent to Wiz Labs or anyone else.

How the app works

You place taps and swipes over another app with a floating control bar and press Play. The app then performs exactly those taps and swipes, at the times you set, using Android's Accessibility service (gesture dispatch). It acts only after you press Play and stops when you press Stop or the Stop button in its notification.

Accessibility service

The Accessibility service is used only to perform the gestures you placed. The app does not read, record or store what is on your screen, does not read text, passwords or notifications, and does not listen to accessibility events or key presses. You can turn the service off at any time in your phone's Accessibility settings.

What is stored on your phone

  • Scripts: the steps, timing and name of each script you create.
  • Run statistics: counts of taps and runs, and the date, length and end reason of your last runs.
  • Settings: defaults, control bar size and opacity, haptics, and the time you accepted the Accessibility disclosure.

All of this stays on your phone. It may be included in your own Android backup if you have backup turned on. Scripts leave your phone only when you export one yourself with the file picker, and you choose where the file goes. Importing a script reads only the file you select.

Permissions

The app asks for notifications (Android 13 and later) so a Stop button can be shown while a script runs. It has no internet, location, contacts, storage, microphone or camera permission.

Google Play components

The app uses Google Play In-App Updates and In-App Review, which are handled by the Google Play Store app under Google's Privacy Policy. This version contains no ads and no analytics or crash-reporting service.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
